## Research & Open Source

### [Papilon](https://github.com/Bodhi8/papilon)
Open-source platform for marketing analytics and simulation. Modules for causal inference, media mix optimization, and system dynamics modeling, enabling simulation-driven strategic planning.

### [Memory-Node Encapsulation (MNE)](https://github.com/Bodhi8/Memory-Node-Encapsulation-MNE-Artificial-Episodic-Memory-and-Cognitive-AI-Reasoning)
Novel cognitive AI architecture enabling artificial episodic memory. Unlike traditional RAG systems, MNE captures complete experiential memories as multidimensional objects with semantic content, emotional valence, and graph-based relational links.

**Benchmarks vs. traditional RAG:**
| Metric | RAG Baseline | MNE System |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| User Preference Recall | 14% | 92% |
| Narrative Consistency | 27% | 88% |
| Zero-Shot Learning | 6% | 74% |

### [The Autonomy Economy](https://github.com/Bodhi8/autonomy-economy)
Comprehensive macroeconomic framework for post-employment civilization powered by AI and automation. Includes Python-based simulation tools for modeling productivity gains, price deflation effects, and policy scenarios.

**Key insight:** Automation creates compound prosperity through simultaneous productivity gains AND price deflation, projecting 5-6x prosperity improvement rather than the 2-3x typically modeled.
